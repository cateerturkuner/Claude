These four stages describe different deals, and will not tie to each other. Leads are enquiries received that month; tours are tours that took place that month, from leads of any month; contracts are contracts signed that month, whenever the event falls; events are events held that month, which is where revenue is recognised. Conversion rates are the proforma's own same-month ratios — a pace measure, not a cohort measure.

Reported months show the actual with its variance underneath; months not yet closed show the proforma in grey.
