# Pro Forma walkthrough: Pre-LOI talking points

1. Frame the venue
   - Market: current vs. new
   - Performing vs. growth: established strong performer, or upside we can unlock
   - Vendor services: what they offer today vs. what we bring, how fast, plus our marketing
   - Employee structure: over/understaffed, owner hands-on or hands-off
2. Key terms
   - Y1 starts the day we close
   - OC events: booked by sellers before close, occur post-close. Honored as booked. Mostly Y1, some Y2, rarely Y3
   - Event vs. contract: events are when they occur, contracts are when they book. Pre-LOI we project events only
3. Market
   - In hub range: under 90 min from each hub (catering, floral, bakery). Dallas: The Olana plus one of three catering hubs. Model on our ramp
   - Outside hub range: in the region geographically and operationally, but too far for hubs. Do we grow that way and add hubs?
   - New market: full new market for Walters. Venue stands alone; vendor services years out
   - Perfect new-market venue: ~100 events/yr, all-inclusive package, owner not involved
4. Top-line projections
   - Three drivers: # of events, % attachment, $ per event
   - Events: historical average, last 12 months, booked next 12
   - By type: Bar license switch, Catering gradual, Floral/Bakery hub-dependent, Photo/DJ/Stationery quick
   - Ramp: typically 30 / 60 / 100% of Walters average by Y3
   - $ per vendor service: Walters average, adjust down for small venues, sanity-check vs. couple spend
   - Don't Walterize too fast (verbal only)
5. Expenses
   - COGS: driven by their offerings; keep existing costs in mind; often none today, so build on our ramp and margins
   - Payroll: current staff, current or new market, owner involvement and replacement cost
   - OpEx: our marketing (higher), their utilities and maintenance per event, our insurance, finance, professional services
