# Pro Forma walkthrough: talking points

## Part 1: Pre-LOI

1. Frame the venue
   - Market: current or new?
   - Performing today, or growth opportunity?
   - Vendor services: today vs. what we bring, how fast, plus our marketing
   - Employee structure: over/understaffed, owner involvement
2. Terms
   - Y1 starts the day we close
   - OC events: booked before close, honored as-is. Mostly Y1, some Y2, rarely Y3
   - Events vs. contracts: events occur in the year; contracts are booked in the year. Pre-LOI we only project events
3. Market
   - In hub range: under 90 min from each hub. Dallas: The Olana plus one of three catering hubs
   - Outside hub range: in the region, grouped operationally, but hub services start at $0. Do we grow that way and open hubs?
   - New market: new region for Walters, venue stands alone, vendor services years out
   - Perfect new-market venue: ~100 events/yr, all-inclusive package, owner not involved (verbal only)
4. Top-line projections
   - Three drivers: # of events, % attachment, $ / event
   - Events: historical average, LTM vs. NTM
   - By type: Bar (any market, license switch), Catering / Floral / Bakery (existing markets, hub distance), Photo / DJ / Stationery (any market, hire and implement)
   - Ramp: typically 30 / 60 / 100% of Walters average by Y3
   - $ per vendor service: Walters average, tweak down for small venues, sanity-check vs. bride spend (verbal only)
   - Don't Walterize too fast (verbal only)
5. Expenses
   - COGS: historically theirs, go-forward ours; carry OC costs (e.g. required caterer)
   - Payroll: current staff, current or new market, owner involvement and replacement cost
   - OpEx: venue-specific (utilities, maintenance) vs. Walters operational changes (marketing, insurance, finance, professional services)

## Part 2: Post-LOI

6. Same process, more detail
   - Rerun everything in Part 1 with updated event counts, financials, and diligence learnings
   - In the weeds: employees role by role and owner replacement; lingering OC COGS; go-forward pricing; attach timeline per vendor service
7. New step: leads / tours / contracts
   - Build backwards from events: events set contracts, T->C sets tours, L->T sets leads
   - Annual first, Y1 to Y3
   - Conversion rates: venue history if they have it (often they don't), otherwise Walters averages
8. Monthly build
   - Annual totals split by month on seasonality; Walters comparable venue if no history
   - Early months ramp to a stable lead and tour count, then contracts follow
   - Events by month: OC land in their booked month, new events fill the remainder
   - % attachment and $ / event applied directly by month, not split from annual
